# Contributing

Thanks for helping make this the best place to follow Flipper One! 🙌

There are two ways to add something:

1. **Open an issue** with the [Submit a project](https://github.com/haxdoggy/Flipper-One/issues/new?template=submit-project.yml) form. This is the easiest route, and a maintainer will add it for you.
2. **Send a pull request** that edits `README.md` directly, following the rules below.

Spotted a spec, price or date that changed? Use the [News or spec update](https://github.com/haxdoggy/Flipper-One/issues/new?template=news-or-spec-update.yml) form, and please include a source.

## What belongs here

- Projects built **for** Flipper One, Flipper OS or FlipCTL: apps, tools, emulators, firmware forks, modules, cases, scripts.
- Work on the **RK3576** or the **RP2350** that matters for Flipper One, like mainline kernel work, NPU support or board ports.
- Guides, videos, talks and articles that add something new.
- Official news, dev logs and campaign milestones.

## What doesn't

- Projects with no README, or a README that doesn't explain what the project does.
- **Prebuilt executables, installers or disk images from unverified sources.** Projects can ship releases, but the source has to be public and reviewable.
- Malware, credential stealers, or anything built to attack people or networks without permission.
- Flipper Zero projects. Try [awesome-flipperzero](https://github.com/djsime1/awesome-flipperzero) instead.
- Duplicates, link farms, and SEO or affiliate links.

## Format

Add your entry to the section where it fits best, at the bottom of that section:

```markdown
- **[Project name](https://link)**: one or two sentences on what it does and why it's cool. *by github-handle*
```

- Describe it in your own words. Keep it short, specific and free of marketing fluff.
- Use the project's real name, and link to the repository or the official page.
- Say if it's early, a proof of concept, or simulation-only.
- One project per pull request makes reviews fast.

## Facts and sources

Specs and dates change all the time while the device is in development. Any change to the **Specs**, **Launch tracker** or **Timeline** sections has to link a source in the pull request: the official [Developer Portal](https://docs.flipper.net/one), the [Flipper blog](https://blog.flipper.net), the Kickstarter page, or reputable press coverage.

## Code of conduct

Be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussions on topic. This is a community project, not affiliated with Flipper Devices.
